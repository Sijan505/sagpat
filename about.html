हाम्रो बारेमाAbout Sagpat

सगपात नेपाली किसानको ताजा उत्पादन सिधै तपाईंको घरसम्म पुर्‍याउँछ।

The name comes from saag-paat, the greens and vegetables every Nepali kitchen starts the day with. We started Sagpat because buying good vegetables in the city meant an early trip to Kalimati, and most people don't have that time.

Every morning we buy from farmers and small dairies we know in Kavre, Dhading, Bhaktapur and Chitwan. Fruit comes from growers in Mustang and Syangja. Everything is sorted and packed at our store in Kalimati and goes out on scooters the same day.

Each product page tells you which district it comes from. If something isn't as fresh as it should be, tell the rider or call us and we'll replace it.

What we promise

  • Fresh, or we replace it
    No forms, just call within 24 hours.
  • Fair prices for farmers
    We buy direct and pay on time.
  • Less plastic
    Paper bags and crates we collect on the next delivery.
