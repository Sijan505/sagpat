गोपनीयता नीतिPrivacy Policy

We only collect what we need to deliver your order, and we never sell your information.

What we collect

  • Your Google account basics when you sign in with Google: your name, email address and profile picture. We don't get your Google password or access to anything else in your Google account.
  • Delivery details you type at checkout: name, phone number, address, landmark and any delivery notes.
  • Your orders: what you bought, when, the price, the payment method and the delivery status.

How we use it

  • To deliver your orders and call you about them.
  • To show your order history and send you notifications about your orders on this website.
  • To keep records our business is required to keep, such as invoices.

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't use it for advertising. Our staff only see what they need for their job (for example, delivery riders see your address and phone number).

Where it's stored

Your information is stored securely with the services that run this website: Vercel (hosting), Turso (database) and Google (sign-in). Your cart and wishlist are kept in your own browser.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time. To see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to delete your account, contact us at our support email or call our phone number. You can also remove Sagpat's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page. Last updated: 21 November 2026.
